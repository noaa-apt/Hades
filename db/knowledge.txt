# Science & Space
speed of light: About 186,282 miles per second (299,792 km/s) in a vacuum.
how far is the sun: About 93 million miles (150 million km) from Earth on average.
how far is the moon: About 238,855 miles (384,400 km) from Earth on average.
how old is the universe: About 13.8 billion years old.
how old is the earth: About 4.5 billion years old.
what is dna: DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) is the molecule that carries genetic instructions for all living organisms.
what is a black hole: A region of space where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape past the event horizon.
what causes thunder: The rapid heating and expansion of air around a lightning bolt creates a sonic shockwave.
why is the sky blue: Rayleigh scattering. Sunlight hits air molecules and blue light scatters more than other colors.
why is the ocean salty: Rainwater erodes salts from rocks, rivers carry them to the ocean, and the water evaporates leaving the salt behind over billions of years.
what is gravity: A fundamental force that attracts objects with mass toward each other.
what is quantum physics: The branch of physics dealing with matter and energy at the smallest scales, where particles behave in ways that defy everyday intuition.
what causes earthquakes: Movement of tectonic plates grinding or slipping against each other, releasing energy as seismic waves.
what causes volcanoes: Molten rock (magma) from the mantle pushing up through cracks in the crust, usually at plate boundaries.
boiling point of water: 212 degrees Fahrenheit (100 Celsius) at sea level.
freezing point of water: 32 degrees Fahrenheit (0 Celsius) at sea level.
how many bones in the human body: 206 in an adult.
how many planets: 8 in our solar system: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
largest planet: Jupiter.
smallest planet: Mercury.
what is a lightyear: The distance light travels in one year, about 5.88 trillion miles (9.46 trillion km).
what is the milky way: The galaxy we live in. A spiral galaxy containing hundreds of billions of stars including our Sun.
what is pi: Pi (3.14159...) is the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter. It never repeats.
pythagorean theorem: In a right triangle: a squared + b squared = c squared, where c is the hypotenuse.
what is photosynthesis: Process by which green plants and some other organisms use sunlight to synthesize foods from carbon dioxide and water.
what is evolution: The process by which different kinds of living organisms developed and diversified from earlier forms during the history of the Earth (natural selection is a key mechanism).
what is the big bang: The leading scientific theory for the origin of the universe, starting from an extremely hot and dense state about 13.8 billion years ago.
what is a nebula: A giant cloud of dust and gas in space, often a region where stars are born.
what is a supernova: The explosion of a star at the end of its life cycle, briefly outshining an entire galaxy.
what is the speed of sound: About 767 mph (1,235 km/h) in dry air at sea level at 20°C.
what is absolute zero: The lowest possible temperature, 0 Kelvin (-273.15°C or -459.67°F), where molecular motion stops.
what is the periodic table: Arrangement of chemical elements ordered by atomic number, showing periodic trends.
how many elements: 118 known elements.
what is an atom: The basic unit of a chemical element, consisting of a nucleus (protons + neutrons) surrounded by electrons.
what is a molecule: A group of atoms bonded together.
what is the greenhouse effect: Trapping of heat in Earth's atmosphere by greenhouse gases like CO2, water vapor, and methane.
what causes seasons: Earth's axial tilt of about 23.5 degrees as it orbits the Sun.
what is a comet: Icy body that releases gas and dust when near the Sun, forming a coma and sometimes a tail.
what is an asteroid: Rocky body orbiting the Sun, mostly in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter.
what is a meteor: A meteoroid that enters Earth's atmosphere and burns up (shooting star). If it hits the ground it's a meteorite.
distance to nearest star: Proxima Centauri is about 4.24 light-years away.
what is the andromeda galaxy: Nearest major galaxy to the Milky Way, about 2.5 million light-years away. On a collision course with us in ~4.5 billion years.
how many stars in the milky way: Estimated 100–400 billion.
what is dark matter: Invisible form of matter that makes up most of the mass in the universe, detected through gravitational effects.
what is dark energy: Mysterious force causing the accelerated expansion of the universe.
what is e=mc2: Einstein's mass-energy equivalence formula. Energy equals mass times the speed of light squared.
what is the doppler effect: Change in frequency of a wave for an observer moving relative to its source (e.g., redshift in astronomy).
what is a virus: Infectious agent that can only replicate inside living cells of organisms.
what is bacteria: Single-celled microorganisms, some beneficial, some pathogenic.
what is the human genome: The complete set of DNA in a human, containing about 3 billion base pairs.
how many chromosomes in humans: 46 (23 pairs).
what is blood type: Classification based on antigens on red blood cells: A, B, AB, O, plus Rh positive/negative.
largest organ in human body: Skin.
strongest muscle: Masseter (jaw) or tongue depending on definition.
how many senses: Traditionally 5 (sight, hearing, taste, smell, touch). More modernly up to 20+.
what is the pH scale: Measures acidity/alkalinity from 0 to 14. 7 is neutral.
what is photosynthesis equation: 6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy → C6H12O6 + 6O2.
what is cellular respiration: Process cells use to break down glucose for energy: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + ATP.
speed of earth rotation: About 1,000 mph (1,600 km/h) at the equator.
speed of earth orbit: About 67,000 mph (107,000 km/h) around the Sun.
what is a blackbody: Idealized object that absorbs all radiation and emits based only on temperature.
what is entropy: Measure of disorder or randomness in a system (second law of thermodynamics).
what is the uncertainty principle: Heisenberg's principle: you cannot precisely know both position and momentum of a particle at the same time.
what is schrodinger's cat: Thought experiment illustrating quantum superposition and measurement problem.
what is the standard model: Theory describing three of the four fundamental forces and classifying elementary particles.
fundamental forces: Gravity, electromagnetism, strong nuclear force, weak nuclear force.
what is a quark: Elementary particle and fundamental constituent of matter (up, down, charm, strange, top, bottom).
what is a lepton: Elementary particle (electron, muon, tau, and their neutrinos).
what is the higgs boson: Particle associated with the Higgs field that gives mass to other particles. Discovered 2012.

# History

who invented the internet: ARPANET was developed by DARPA in the late 60s. Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web in 1989.
who invented electricity: Nobody invented it, it's natural. Edison and Tesla made it usable. Franklin studied it.
who invented the telephone: Alexander Graham Bell, patented in 1876.
who invented the light bulb: Thomas Edison in 1879.
who invented the computer: Many people. Babbage designed early mechanical versions. Turing laid the theory. ENIAC (1945) was one of the first electronic general-purpose computers.
who invented the airplane: Orville and Wilbur Wright, first powered flight in 1903.
who is albert einstein: German-born physicist (1879-1955). Developed the theory of relativity. Nobel Prize in Physics 1921.
who is nikola tesla: Serbian-American inventor (1856-1943). Pioneered AC electricity, the Tesla coil, and contributed to radio.
who is isaac newton: English physicist and mathematician (1643-1727). Laws of motion and gravity, invented calculus.
who is galileo: Italian astronomer and physicist (1564-1642). Supported heliocentrism, improved the telescope.
who is charles darwin: English naturalist (1809-1882). Developed theory of evolution by natural selection.
who is marie curie: Polish-French physicist and chemist (1867-1934). Discovered radioactivity, first woman to win Nobel Prize, only person to win in two sciences.
who is leonardo da vinci: Italian polymath (1452-1519). Artist, inventor, scientist. Painted Mona Lisa and The Last Supper.
who is shakespeare: English playwright and poet (1564-1616). Wrote Hamlet, Romeo and Juliet, Macbeth, etc.
who is julius caesar: Roman general and statesman (100–44 BC). Key figure in the fall of the Roman Republic.
who is cleopatra: Last active pharaoh of Ancient Egypt (69–30 BC). Of the Ptolemaic dynasty.
who is genghis khan: Founder of the Mongol Empire (c. 1162–1227). Created the largest contiguous empire in history.
who is alexander the great: King of Macedon (356–323 BC). Created one of the largest empires of the ancient world.
who is napoleon: French military leader and emperor (1769–1821). Rose during the French Revolution.
who is abraham lincoln: 16th President of the United States (1809–1865). Led during Civil War, abolished slavery.
who is george washington: 1st President of the United States (1732–1799). Commander of Continental Army.
who is martin luther king jr: American civil rights leader (1929–1968). Famous for "I Have a Dream" speech.
who is mahatma gandhi: Leader of Indian independence movement (1869–1948). Advocated nonviolent resistance.
who is nelson mandela: South African anti-apartheid revolutionary and president (1918–2013).
when did the roman empire fall: Western Roman Empire traditionally 476 AD. Eastern (Byzantine) fell 1453.
when did the industrial revolution start: Late 18th century in Britain.
when was the american revolution: 1775–1783.
when was the french revolution: 1789–1799.
when was the civil war: American Civil War 1861–1865.
when did the cold war end: 1991 with the dissolution of the Soviet Union.
when was the berlin wall built: 1961.
when did the berlin wall fall: November 9, 1989.
when was the first moon landing: July 20, 1969 (Apollo 11, Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin).
who was the first man on the moon: Neil Armstrong.
who was the first woman in space: Valentina Tereshkova (1963).
who was the first man in space: Yuri Gagarin (1961).
when was the printing press invented: Around 1440 by Johannes Gutenberg.
when was gunpowder invented: 9th century China.
when was the wheel invented: Around 3500 BC in Mesopotamia.
when was writing invented: Around 3200 BC in Sumer (cuneiform).
ancient wonders of the world: Great Pyramid of Giza, Hanging Gardens of Babylon, Temple of Artemis, Statue of Zeus, Mausoleum at Halicarnassus, Colossus of Rhodes, Lighthouse of Alexandria.

# Technology
what is bitcoin: A decentralized digital currency created in 2009 by Satoshi Nakamoto. Uses blockchain, no central bank.
what is ai: Artificial Intelligence. Computer systems designed to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
what is machine learning: A subset of AI where systems learn from data to improve over time without being explicitly programmed for every task.
what is blockchain: A distributed digital ledger recording transactions across many computers, making records very hard to alter.
what is the cloud: Servers and services accessed over the internet rather than on your local hardware.
what is an api: Application Programming Interface. Rules letting different software programs communicate with each other.
what is open source: Software whose source code is publicly available for anyone to view, use, modify, and distribute.
what is linux: An open-source OS kernel created by Linus Torvalds in 1991. Android and most web servers run on it.
what is windows: Microsoft's commercial OS, the most widely used desktop OS in the world.
what is python: A high-level programming language known for clean syntax. Popular in AI, data science, and automation.
what is javascript: A programming language that runs in web browsers and on servers. The backbone of interactive web content.
what is html: HyperText Markup Language. Standard language for creating web pages.
what is css: Cascading Style Sheets. Used to style and layout web pages.
what is sql: Structured Query Language. Used to manage and query relational databases.
what is a cpu: Central Processing Unit. The "brain" of a computer that executes instructions.
what is a gpu: Graphics Processing Unit. Specialized for parallel processing, used in graphics and AI.
what is ram: Random Access Memory. Temporary fast storage for data the computer is actively using.
what is an ssd: Solid State Drive. Storage device with no moving parts, faster than traditional hard drives.
what is the internet: Global network of interconnected computers using standardized protocols (TCP/IP).
what is wifi: Wireless networking technology based on IEEE 802.11 standards.
what is bluetooth: Short-range wireless technology for connecting devices.
what is 5g: Fifth generation of cellular network technology, offering higher speeds and lower latency.
what is virtual reality: Computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional environment experienced via headset.
what is augmented reality: Technology that overlays digital information onto the real world.
what is cryptocurrency: Digital or virtual currency secured by cryptography, typically decentralized.
what is nft: Non-Fungible Token. Unique digital asset verified on a blockchain.
what is the metaverse: Shared virtual spaces, often accessed via VR/AR, where users can interact.
what is quantum computing: Computing using quantum-mechanical phenomena (superposition, entanglement) for potentially massive speedups on certain problems.
what is deep learning: Subset of machine learning using neural networks with many layers.
what is a neural network: Computing system inspired by biological neural networks, used in AI.
what is chatgpt: AI chatbot developed by OpenAI based on large language models.
what is github: Platform for version control and collaborative software development using Git.
what is docker: Platform for developing, shipping, and running applications in containers.
what is kubernetes: Open-source system for automating deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications.
what is tcp/ip: Fundamental suite of protocols that powers the internet.
what is http: HyperText Transfer Protocol. Foundation of data communication on the World Wide Web.
what is https: Secure version of HTTP using encryption (TLS/SSL).
what is a firewall: Network security system that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing traffic.
what is malware: Malicious software designed to harm or exploit devices/networks.
what is phishing: Fraudulent attempt to obtain sensitive information by disguising as a trustworthy entity.
what is two factor authentication: Security process requiring two different forms of identification.
what is encryption: Process of converting information into a code to prevent unauthorized access.
what is a vpn: Virtual Private Network. Creates a secure, encrypted connection over a less secure network.
what is the dark web: Part of the internet that requires special software (like Tor) to access and is not indexed by search engines.
what is tor: Free software for enabling anonymous communication.
what is a server: Computer or system that provides resources, data, or services to other computers (clients).
what is a client: Device or program that requests services from a server.
what is bandwidth: Maximum rate of data transfer across a network path.
what is latency: Delay before a transfer of data begins following an instruction.
what is a pixel: Smallest unit of a digital image or display.
what is resolution: Number of pixels in each dimension that can be displayed.
what is openai: AI research company that developed GPT models and ChatGPT.
what is google: American multinational technology company specializing in search, advertising, cloud, and AI.
what is apple: American technology company known for iPhone, Mac, iPad, and services.
what is microsoft: American technology company known for Windows, Office, Azure, and Xbox.
what is amazon: American multinational technology and e-commerce company; also major cloud provider (AWS).
what is meta: Parent company of Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp (formerly Facebook Inc.).
what is tesla: American electric vehicle and clean energy company founded by Elon Musk and others.
what is spacex: American aerospace manufacturer and space transport company founded by Elon Musk.
what is x: Social media platform formerly known as Twitter, owned by Elon Musk / xAI related entities.
what is elon musk: Entrepreneur associated with Tesla, SpaceX, Neuralink, The Boring Company, xAI, and owner of X.

# Math
what is algebra: Branch of mathematics dealing with symbols and the rules for manipulating them.
what is calculus: Mathematical study of continuous change (differentiation and integration).
what is geometry: Branch of mathematics concerned with properties of space, shapes, and sizes.
what is trigonometry: Study of relationships between angles and sides of triangles.
what is statistics: Science of collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data.
what is probability: Measure of the likelihood that an event will occur (between 0 and 1).
what is fibonacci sequence: Series where each number is the sum of the two preceding ones: 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13...
what is the golden ratio: Approximately 1.618, often appearing in nature and art (phi).
what is infinity: Concept of something without bound or end.
what is a prime number: Natural number greater than 1 with no positive divisors other than 1 and itself.
what is the quadratic formula: For ax² + bx + c = 0, x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / (2a).
what is e: Euler's number, approximately 2.71828, base of natural logarithms.
what is the order of operations: PEMDAS / BODMAS (Parentheses/Brackets, Exponents/Orders, Multiplication-Division, Addition-Subtraction).
what is a fraction: Number representing part of a whole (numerator / denominator).
what is a percentage: Number or ratio expressed as a fraction of 100.
what is the area of a circle: πr².
what is the circumference of a circle: 2πr.
what is the volume of a sphere: (4/3)πr³.
what is the area of a triangle: (1/2) × base × height.
what is the pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c² for right triangles.
